# Shadow AI Discovery Plan

## Shadow AI Definition

Shadow AI means AI tools used by staff without formal review, approval, or governance oversight.

**Examples:**

- Staff using personal AI accounts for museum work
- Uploading object records into public AI tools
- Using unapproved translation tools for exhibition labels
- Using AI note-takers in sensitive meetings
- Using AI image recognition without rights review

## Shadow AI Discovery Methods

1. Department self-reporting survey
2. IT software usage review
3. Procurement records review
4. Staff interviews
5. Browser extension review
6. Training survey
7. Review of AI-generated content workflows
8. Review of vendor invoices and subscriptions

## Shadow AI Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Confidential data uploaded to unapproved AI tool | High | Data handling policy |
| Public content generated without review | High | Publication approval workflow |
| AI output enters official records | High | Records review requirement |
| Staff use personal accounts | Medium | Approved tool list |
| Unknown vendor data use | High | Vendor governance review |
