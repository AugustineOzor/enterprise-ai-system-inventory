# AI System Inventory Findings Report

## Summary

The initial AI inventory identified 18 AI-enabled systems across museum departments.

## Key Findings

1. Seven systems are high-risk due to public-facing output, cultural heritage impact, or official-record impact.
2. Four shadow AI tools were identified.
3. One system had no assigned business owner.
4. Three public-facing AI systems require enhanced monitoring.
5. Two systems may process confidential or restricted information and require immediate review.

## Recommended Actions

1. Assign owners to all AI systems.
2. Suspend unapproved tools handling sensitive data.
3. Require governance review for all public-facing AI.
4. Add vendor AI tools to procurement review.
5. Update the inventory dashboard monthly.
