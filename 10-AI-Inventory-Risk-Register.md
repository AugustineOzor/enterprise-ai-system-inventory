# Risk Register — AI Inventory Program

Risks associated with running the AI inventory program itself (not risks of individual AI systems — see [Shadow AI Discovery Plan](Shadow-AI-Discovery-Plan.md) for that register).

| Risk ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Rating | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | AI systems are used without being inventoried | High | Medium | High | Mandatory intake process |
| R-02 | No business owner is assigned | High | Medium | High | Ownership requirement |
| R-03 | System risk tier is misclassified | High | Medium | High | Governance review |
| R-04 | Shadow AI remains undiscovered | High | Medium | High | Department survey and IT review |
| R-05 | Public-facing AI is not reviewed | High | Low | Medium | Public AI approval workflow |
| R-06 | Restricted data is used in AI tools | High | Medium | High | Data classification controls |
| R-07 | Inventory becomes outdated | Medium | High | High | Review cadence |
| R-08 | Vendor AI tools are missed | Medium | Medium | Medium | Procurement integration |
| R-09 | Human review requirements are unclear | High | Medium | High | Inventory field and policy |
| R-10 | Dashboard metrics are not maintained | Medium | Medium | Medium | Governance reporting cadence |
