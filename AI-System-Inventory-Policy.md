# AI System Inventory Policy

All AI systems used by the museum must be recorded in the enterprise AI system inventory before they are purchased, piloted, deployed, or used for official work.

Each AI system must include:

- Business owner
- System owner
- Department
- Business purpose
- Vendor or provider
- Data used
- Data classification
- Public-facing status
- Human oversight requirement
- Risk tier
- Approval status
- Monitoring requirement
- Review date

AI systems that are not recorded in the inventory are considered unapproved until reviewed.
